Get my QR - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Get my QR does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything happens on your device. The extension makes no network requests, contains no analytics or advertising, and does not require an account.

What the extension does with your data

  • When you click the toolbar icon, press the shortcut, or use the right-click menu, it reads the address, title and icon of that tab (or the link or text you chose) to build the QR code.
  • The QR code is drawn locally in the popup. Exports (PNG, SVG, clipboard) are created locally and saved to your device or clipboard only when you ask.
  • Your style, color and toggle choices are saved with the browser's own storage so they sync across your signed-in browsers. We never see them.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionReason
activeTabRead the URL and title of the page you want a QR code for, only after you invoke the extension.
scriptingRead the page's theme-color meta tag, once per click on the active tab, to match the QR code to the site's brand color.
storageRemember your style and settings on your device.
contextMenusAdd "Get my QR" entries to the right-click menu.
favicon (Chrome and Edge)Show the site's icon in the popup and in the center of the QR code.

Third parties

None. QR codes are static: the address is encoded directly in the image, and scans never pass through any server operated by us.

Children

The extension collects no data from anyone, including children.
